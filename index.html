Your child's first language starts at your kitchen table.

A working prototype. Type a word and see the sign. Practise with the camera. Ask the guide a question. Everything on this page is real code, and where something is not built yet it says so rather than pretending.

1 Type a word, see the sign

Type anything. The app looks the word up and plays the real sign.

Why the sign is filmed, not drawn. BSL is not English in a different order - it has its own grammar, and much of that grammar lives in the face and body, not only the hands. An AI asked to generate a sign will produce something confident and wrong. So the cartoon is the guide, and the signing is always real footage of a real signer - which, here, means Justine.

2 Practise with the camera

Hand tracking runs on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and no video leaves your phone or laptop.

SHOW ME hello

Press start and allow the camera.

What this can honestly do. The camera tracks the position of the hands, live, in the browser. That is enough to see a hand, count it, and tell whether it is being held steady.

What it cannot do - and this matters. It does not understand BSL. Nobody has a system that reliably recognises arbitrary signing from a webcam, and any product claiming it is claiming something untrue. So the game here is a closed set: the app asks for one sign it has just shown you, and one day will check your handshape against Justine's own filmed version of that same sign. That is a matching job, not understanding - and it is the only honest version.

3 Ask the guide

This one is not scripted. It is a real Hermes agent answering, with a brief it is not allowed to break.

Justine's guideHello. I can explain how BSL works, what to expect in a first lesson, and what to say to your child's school. I cannot show you signs myself - for that, watch Justine. Ask me anything.
The guide's rules. It is a real AI agent, driven by Hermes, with its full toolkit behind it. It is instructed: never claim to be Justine; never claim to be a person; never invent a sign or describe a sign in words as if that teaches it; always send sign questions to Justine's real videos. On a product for deaf children, those rules are the product.
Justine BSL - prototype. Built to be looked at, not shipped. Real: the sign lookup, the hand tracking, the agent. Not real yet: the sign footage (awaiting Justine's clips), the handshape templates for scoring, accounts, payments and progress saving. Sign video is currently a labelled placeholder, and the camera check scores steadiness only.
